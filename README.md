# ☕ Coffee Retail Sales & Customer Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee retail sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, product performance, customer behavior, and profitability.

The project was created as a beginner Data Analyst portfolio project to demonstrate the process of transforming raw transaction data into business insights and recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The coffee business wants to understand which products, coffee types, roast types, and markets contribute most to revenue and profitability.

The analysis also evaluates customer loyalty behavior and overall sales performance to identify opportunities for improving business performance.

---

## ❓ Business Questions

The analysis focuses on the following questions:

1. How are sales changing over time?
2. Which coffee types generate the most revenue?
3. Which countries generate the most revenue?
4. Which roast types perform best?
5. Which products are the top performers?
6. How do loyalty and non-loyalty customers compare?
7. How does revenue convert into cost and gross profit?

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared and analyzed in Microsoft Excel.

Key preparation and calculation steps included:

- Checked the transaction data for consistency.
- Connected order information with product and customer information.
- Used Product ID and Customer ID to retrieve related information.
- Calculated revenue using quantity × unit price.
- Calculated total cost using unit cost × quantity.
- Calculated gross profit using revenue − total cost.
- Created Year, Month, and Quarter fields for time-based analysis.
- Built PivotTables and PivotCharts for business analysis.
- Created an interactive dashboard using slicers and a timeline.

## 🔎 Key Insights

### 1. Geographic Performance

The United States generates approximately 79% of total sales, making it the dominant market in the dataset.

### 2. Coffee Type

Excelsa generates the highest sales among the four coffee types, while Robusta generates the lowest.

### 3. Roast Type

Light Roast is the strongest-performing roast category and generates approximately 38% of total sales.

### 4. Product Performance

The highest-selling products are concentrated among the 2.5kg products, making this size an important contributor to revenue.

### 5. Loyalty Customers

Non-loyalty customers account for higher total sales than loyalty customers in the dataset. However, total sales alone cannot determine the effectiveness of the loyalty program.

### 6. Sales Trend

2021 recorded the highest full-year sales in the dataset. The 2022 data only covers January through August, so it should not be directly compared with complete previous years.

### 7. Profitability

The business generated approximately $45.1K in sales and $4.5K in gross profit, resulting in a gross margin of approximately 10%.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

### 1. Geographic Growth

Continue prioritizing the United States while exploring opportunities to increase sales in Ireland and the United Kingdom to reduce dependence on a single market.

### 2. Coffee Type

Prioritize strong-performing coffee types in promotional campaigns while investigating the reasons behind the lower performance of Robusta.

### 3. Roast Type

Maintain sufficient inventory of Light Roast products and investigate whether pricing, availability, or customer preferences contribute to its stronger performance.

### 4. Product Performance

Prioritize high-performing 2.5kg products in inventory planning and promotional activities while monitoring their demand and profitability.

### 5. Loyalty Program

Evaluate the loyalty program using additional metrics such as average order value, purchase frequency, and customer retention before making changes to the program.

### 6. Profitability

Review product pricing and costs to identify opportunities to improve gross margin, especially for high-selling products.

---

## ⚠️ Data Limitations

The dataset contains approximately 1,000 order records.

The profitability analysis is based on the cost and profit information provided in the source dataset.

The 2022 data only covers January through August, so it should not be directly compared with complete previous years.

The analysis identifies relationships and patterns in the dataset but does not establish causation.
